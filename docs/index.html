The problem

Audio works. Control doesn't.

Your headphones pair with your Mac and play perfectly well. But the moment you want to switch noise cancelling off, or pull the bass back, you have to pick up your phone and open the Soundcore app.

Anker only ships that app for Android and iOS. So the controls live on a different device from the one you're listening on.

Install

Three lines.

$ brew tap mervin008/tap
$ brew trust mervin008/tap
$ brew install --cask --no-quarantine mervin008/tap/soundcorebridge

Homebrew asks you to trust third-party casks, hence the middle line. The app isn't notarised by Apple, which is what the last flag handles. Needs macOS 13 or later.

Prefer a download? Take the zip from releases.

Compatibility

One model, properly.

Soundcore Space 2Full control
Other Soundcore modelsBattery and firmware

Only the Space 2 has been tested against real hardware, so it's the only one that gets write access. Everything else stays read-only rather than guessing. If you have another model, open an issue — it's mostly a matter of confirming what it does.
